## What's On Your Dashboard

When you log in, you'll land on your own personal dashboard with **7 tabs** — here's what each one gives you:

---

### 📊 Evaluations

Your main view. Every evaluation you have with us — from Challenge Phase 1 and Phase 2 through to Funded and Farming — laid out in a clean table. You can see:

- **Prop Firm, Account Size, Fees** — all your eval info at a glance
- **Hedge Results** — every hedge result for every phase, automatically matched from live MT5 trades
- **Payouts** — what's come back from funded accounts
- **Farming Results** — daily hedge results tracked across dedicated columns for accounts in the farming phase
- **Status tracking** — see exactly where each evaluation sits (Pass, Fail, In Progress, Funded, etc.)

Use the **section jump buttons** (Info, Eval, Funded, Farm) to skip straight to the columns you care about, and the **Active Only filter** to hide completed or failed evals and focus on what's live.

> 💡 **Sticky Notes** — Right-click any cell to add a personal note. Great for flagging something or leaving yourself a reminder. You can create, edit, and delete notes on any cell.

---

### 📈 Stats

Your financial summary — the same calculations you saw on the old Stats tab, now computed automatically in real time:

- Profitability and cashflow
- Expected value (EV)
- Funded rate
- Average net per evaluation
- Hedge account overview — deposits, withdrawals, balance, and any discrepancies

No more waiting for spreadsheet formulas to catch up. It's instant.

---

### 📉 Profitability Waterlog

Daily profit watermarks — your high and low snapshots tracked over time, plus your full **profit share history**. This gives you a clear picture of how your portfolio is performing across bi-weekly periods.

---

### 🏦 Hedge Accounts

A clean view of your hedge account configurations and **live open positions**. See exactly what's running at any time.

---

### 🏢 Prop Firm Accounts

All your prop firm login details in one place. No more digging through emails or messages to find credentials.

---

### 🖥️ VPS

Your VPS configuration details — server info all in one spot.

---

### 💳 Payment Info

Your billing address and payment card details, stored securely and accessible whenever you need them.
