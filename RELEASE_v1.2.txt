Hey team — we're excited to officially roll out *Tradeopss AI*, your new trading automation companion. This is the tool that handles hedging, syncing, billing, breach detection, and now *automatic status tracking* — all from one desktop app.

---

🤖 *What is Tradeopss AI?*

It's a desktop app that connects your prop firm accounts, MetaTrader 5, and the Tradeopss Dashboard into one workflow. You trade (or let the AI trade for you) — it handles everything else automatically.

---

🏢 *Supported Prop Firms*

> MFFU  •  MFFU Flex  •  Funded Next  •  TopStep  •  Tradeify  •  Lucid Trading  •  FundingTicks  •  TradeDay  •  Alpha Futures (NQ & GC)  •  Apex  •  Top One Futures

That's *10 firms* running in parallel, each in its own browser — no bottlenecks.

---

⚡ *Core Features*

🔀 *Multi-Firm Trade Execution*
Connect all your prop firm accounts at once. Each firm gets its own browser instance — trades fire simultaneously across Tradovate and TopStepX.

📊 *MT5 Hedging Engine*
Every prop firm trade is automatically hedged on MT5 in the opposite direction. TP/SL is calculated based on your current balance so you never overshoot a target or breach a drawdown limit.

⏰ *Auto-Trade Scheduler*
Set it and forget it. The scheduler fires trades with randomized timing, picks direction using 26+ technical indicators, and runs the full trade → hedge → sync pipeline hands-free.

📡 *Signal-Based Direction*
RSI, MACD, Supertrend, Bollinger Bands, ADX, Stochastic, and 20+ more. The signal engine reads live MT5 data and picks the optimal side for each trade.

🛡️ *Hedge Guard (SL Protection)*
Real-time monitoring across MT5 and your broker. SL hit on one side? The other closes instantly. TP hit? The hedge keeps running for a shot at double profit.

🚨 *Automatic Breach Detection*
The AI scrapes your prop firm dashboard on every connection. Breached accounts get flagged immediately on your Tradeopss Dashboard with the specific reason.

💰 *Auto Billing & Fee Tracking*
Challenge fees, purchase dates, and payout amounts scraped automatically from each firm's billing page and synced to your dashboard.

🔄 *Smart Data Sync*
MT5 deal history is parsed, matched to prop accounts via trade comments, and pushed to the dashboard. Only sends when new trades exist — no duplicates.

🎯 *Balance-Aware Trade Sizing*
Close to a target? TP shrinks to land precisely. Farming mode? MT5 TP is capped to prevent breaching the hard-stop threshold.

📋 *Blueprint Stage Tracking*
Challenge → Funded → Double Dip → Farming — the AI knows exactly where each account is and what comes next.

---

✨ *NEW in v1.2 — Auto-Status Tracking*

This is the big one. Accounts now *automatically* update their status:

    ✅  *Pass* — balance hits the profit target
    🔄  *In Progress* — the moment a trade goes out
    🚫  *Fail* — account breaches or balance drops below threshold

No more manually changing statuses on the dashboard. The AI handles it based on live balance data from your prop firm.

🏁 *Getting Started*

1️⃣  Launch `TradeopssAI_v1.2.exe`
2️⃣  Enter your client email → click *Lookup*
3️⃣  Click *Load Trades* to pull your active evaluations
4️⃣  Connect your prop firm browsers (one per firm)
5️⃣  Connect MT5 with your hedging account credentials
6️⃣  Hit *BUY/SELL* manually, or toggle *Auto-Trade*

The AI takes it from there. 🤝
